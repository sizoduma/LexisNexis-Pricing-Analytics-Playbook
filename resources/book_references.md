# Book and resource references

This repository is built around pricing and analytics disciplines that map strongly to the Senior Manager: Pricing & Analytics role at LexisNexis.

## Core pricing analytics books

### 1) Essentials of Pricing Analytics: Tools and Implementation with Excel, R, and Python
Author: Erik Haugom & Per Kristian Alnes

Why it matters:
- bridges Excel-based commercial modeling with Python-based implementation
- directly relevant for pricing analysis, elasticity, and optimization
- ideal for subscription and data-led business pricing questions

Key skills:
- price sensitivity and elasticity
- pricing optimization and segmentation
- implementation patterns in Python and spreadsheet tools

### 2) Digital Pricing Strategy: Capturing Value from Digital Innovations
Author: Stephan M. Liozu & Andreas Hinterhuber

Why it matters:
- focused on digital products, value extraction, and SaaS monetization
- highly relevant for content platforms, data services, and recurring subscriptions
- helps frame pricing as part of customer value strategy rather than cost recovery

Key skills:
- value-based pricing logic
- packaged product design
- platform and subscription strategy

## Customer analytics / predictive modeling books

### 3) Marketing Data Science: Modeling Techniques in Predictive Analytics with R and Python
Author: Thomas W. Miller

Why it matters:
- useful when applying data science to campaigns, demand, retention, and LTV
- especially relevant for offer design and customer value management

Key skills:
- customer analytics and retention modeling
- demand estimation
- campaign evaluation

### 4) Business Analytics: Data Science for Business Problems
Author: Walter R. Paczkowski

Why it matters:
- practical business problem framing across pricing, forecasting, and decisions
- useful for communicating insights to business stakeholders

Key skills:
- business-focused analytics workflows
- demand and pricing case studies
- Python-driven business analysis

## How these map to LexisNexis

The role emphasizes:
- value-based pricing
- campaign and offer design
- churn/retention monitoring
- pricing dashboards and tools
- competitive pricing intelligence
- collaboration with sales, product, and finance

These references collectively support a pricing leader who can operate at the intersection of strategy, analytics, and execution.
