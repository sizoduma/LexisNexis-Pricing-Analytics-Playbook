# LexisNexis Pricing & Analytics Playbook

A practical repository designed to help you prepare for the Senior Manager: Pricing & Analytics role at LexisNexis. It brings together:

- subscription monetization strategy for SaaS and content businesses
- value-based pricing frameworks for legal/professional information products
- Python-driven pricing optimization and elasticity analysis
- customer churn and retention analytics
- dashboard and KPI design for pricing teams
- interview-ready guidance tailored to LexisNexis

## Why this repo matters

LexisNexis operates in a data-rich, subscription-heavy environment where pricing decisions must balance:

- customer value and willingness-to-pay
- regional pricing localization
- promotion and discount governance
- retention, churn, and upsell opportunities
- profitability and market competitiveness

This repository helps translate those concerns into structured frameworks, analytical workflows, and executable examples.

## Interview themes for LexisNexis

Recommended talking points:

1. Position pricing as a strategic growth lever, not just a discounting function.
2. Show how you would segment customers by value, behavior, and market need.
3. Demonstrate how value-based pricing can be applied to legal and professional information products.
4. Discuss retention and churn as commercial levers tied to pricing and packaging choices.
5. Explain how dashboards and AI/automation support commercial decisions at scale.
