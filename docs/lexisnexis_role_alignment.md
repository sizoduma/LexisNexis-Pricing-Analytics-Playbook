# LexisNexis role alignment

## Role summary

The Senior Manager: Pricing & Analytics role sits at the intersection of commercial strategy, customer value, and analytical execution. It is not simply about discounting. It is about designing sustainable pricing systems for a digital information and legal analytics business.

## Strategic fit

### Pricing should be a growth lever
For a business like LexisNexis, pricing can shape:
- customer acquisition
- renewal behavior
- product adoption
- market competitiveness
- gross margin quality

A strong pricing manager must connect product value to customer segments and regional needs without sacrificing profitability.

### What good pricing looks like in this context

- simple customer-friendly value-based offers
- localized pricing and packaging by market
- discount controls and governance
- segmentation by use case, customer size, and product intensity
- metric-driven evaluation of promotional effectiveness

## Core responsibilities mapped to capability areas

### Strategic pricing leadership
- define pricing principles aligned to growth and customer value
- act as commercial partner to sales, finance, and product teams
- influence decision-making at a governance level

### Market-aligned pricing strategy
- create regional and country-level price structures
- design packages for varied customer segments
- balance local relevance with global consistency

### Campaigns and offers
- evaluate promotions and commercial offers
- design price tests and incentives around adoption and expansion
- assess whether a campaign creates durable customer value

### Customer value management
- watch churn, retention, and pricing sensitivity
- identify expansion or cross-sell opportunities
- use pricing design to support retention and long-term value

### Data and analytics
- build dashboards and tools using Excel, Salesforce, Tableau, and Python
- track key metrics such as conversion, retention, and revenue quality
- enable leaders with actionable commercial narratives

## Executive narrative to use in interviews

> I would approach pricing at LexisNexis as a strategic lever for customer value and business growth, not as a static operational exercise. In a subscription and information-led business, the strongest pricing models are those that align packages to customer needs, supporting retention and expansion while maintaining profitability. My approach would combine value-based segmentation, data-driven pricing analysis, and disciplined governance to create price structures that are locally relevant and globally consistent.

## Practical use cases

- define a customer tier strategy for premium legal and business research users
- create offer frameworks for new customer acquisition and cross-sell expansion
- use retention and usage metrics to refine package design
- evaluate discount exceptions against revenue quality and customer lifetime value

The winning formula for this role is simple:

- strategic mindset
- business partnership across teams
- analytic rigor
- value-based pricing logic
- customer and revenue sensitivity awareness
